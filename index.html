CNT/PDMS Composite

RaMS Lab (Dr. Jun Sheng), UCRStudent Undergraduate ResearcherSolo project

Goal: Create a homogeneous composite mixture of carbon nanotubes (CNT) and polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS) for use as a conductive biosensor in soft robotic applications.

What I did

  • Created an SOP to achieve a homogeneous mixture of CNT and PDMS (Fig. 1), plus an SOP for using the sonicator (Q125)
  • Handled chloroform as a universal solvent, mixed via sonication
  • Carefully evaporated the chloroform to reduce the mixture to the desired compounds, then cured it (Fig. 2)

Result: A flexible, conductive biosensor whose resistance reading varies with deformation.
