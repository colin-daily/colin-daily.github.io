    RaMS Lab under Dr. Jun Sheng, UC Riverside

    CNT/PDMS Composite

    RaMS Lab (Dr. Jun Sheng), UCRStudent Undergraduate ResearcherSolo project

    Goal: Create a homogeneous composite mixture of carbon nanotubes (CNT) and polydimethylsiloxane (PDMS) for use as a conductive biosensor in soft robotic applications.

    What I did

    • Created an SOP to achieve a homogeneous mixture of CNT and PDMS (Fig. 1), plus an SOP for using the sonicator (Q125)
    • Handled chloroform as a universal solvent, mixed via sonication
    • Carefully evaporated the chloroform to reduce the mixture to the desired compounds, then cured it (Fig. 2)

    Result: A flexible, conductive biosensor whose resistance reading varies with deformation.

    • Sonication
    • Solvent Evaporation
    • UV Curing
    • Electrical Conductivity Testing

    Biomechanical Analysis of Smart Hydrogels for Tendon Repair

    Biomechanics of the Human Body, UCRStudentPart of a 4-person team

    Goal: Compare hydrogel compositions and validate improvements in stress distribution.

    What I did

    • Created 3D CAD models of collagen-based hydrogels to simulate tendon repair scaffolds (Fig. 1)
    • Ran COMSOL Multiphysics simulations of stress-strain behavior and swelling under physiological conditions
    • Evaluated Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio and diffusion coefficient to assess hydrogel performance

    Goal: Investigate tumor-induced blood-flow changes and their implications for chemotherapy effectiveness.

    What I did

    • Designed 3D CAD models of carotid artery bifurcations with varying tumor sizes (Fig. 1)
